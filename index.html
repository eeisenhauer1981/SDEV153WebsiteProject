Welcome to In My Kitchen!

Your home for recipe storage and easy meal planning

Build a recipe book with recipes from your favorite websites and blogs. Find a recipe you like, add its name, a link, and a photo, and save it to your recipe list!

Make a weekly meal plan with the menu planner. Enter the number of breakfasts, lunches, and dinners you want to plan. The menu planner randomly picks meals from your recipe book to create a unique meal plan.
